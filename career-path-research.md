# Career Path Research: Penetration Testing

## Why Cybersecurity
No mandatory degree or long licensing process required. The field is open to
people from any background — what matters most is curiosity, problem-solving,
and staying calm under pressure, not a computer science degree.

## Career Quiz Result: Penetration Tester
A penetration tester ("pentester" or "ethical hacker") is hired by an
organization to safely try to break into their own systems, just like a real
attacker would — but under agreed rules, and with the goal of finding and
reporting weaknesses before real attackers do.

### Responsibilities
- Test the security of computer systems, networks, and web applications
- Perform security assessments, audits, and analyze security policies
- Analyze results and write reports advising how to prevent an attack

## Why This Fits
The Offensive Security Intro room I completed (finding hidden pages with
DirBuster, attacking the admin page) was a direct, hands-on taste of exactly
this role.

## What I Learned
Cybersecurity has 50+ distinct roles (SOC analyst, incident responder, threat
intel researcher, etc.), and penetration testing specifically suits people who
like thinking like an attacker to find weaknesses before they're exploited.
